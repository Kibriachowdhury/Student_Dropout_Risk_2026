# Student Dropout Risk Analysis (2026)

This repository contains the Jupyter Notebook for the **Foundations of Computer Science** project, analyzing student dropout patterns, academic trends, risk scores, and country-level GDP per capita data.

## Project Overview
The analysis in `Student_Dropout_Risk_2026.ipynb` covers:
- **At-Risk Online Students:** Filtering online learners with low attendance and missed deadlines, and computing dropout rates by course type.
- **Academic Performance & Integrity Checks:** Computing pass rates, semester-to-semester GPA changes/trends, and verifying enrolled vs. passed/failed unit counts.
- **Workload & Risk Scoring:** Grouping students by weekly work hours and constructing a composite dropout risk score.
- **GDP & Continent-Level Analysis:** Cleaning World Bank GDP per capita data (Our World in Data), merging it with student nationality records, and computing dropout rates across continents.
- **LLM Disclosure:** Includes a dedicated section documenting the targeted use of Large Language Models during development.

## Repository Contents
- `Student_Dropout_Risk_2026.ipynb`: Complete code, outputs, and explanations for all project tasks.
